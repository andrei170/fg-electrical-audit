Twenty-Four Ways To Ring You, And Nothing Counting Who Does.

What You Told Our Team
 What you said
Testing work20 to 25 a week, and you would happily take double that or more
EV chargersYou do them, but the usual flow feels like it is slowing
Team3 employees on the books, normally 2 to 3 subcontractors as well
The callFirst dial that connected, and you booked
⭐ Two different asks in one sentence. Doubling testing work is a volume problem. EV flow slowing is a measurement problem, because without tracking nobody can tell you whether it is actually slowing or just feels that way.
What We Found
🔴🔴 You have no tracking of any kind

No Google Analytics, no Tag Manager, no advertising tag, no pixel. Nothing.

⭐⭐ Which is why the EV question cannot be answered. You said the flow feels like it is slowing. Nothing on your site can confirm or deny that, so you are navigating on a feeling. It might be slowing, it might be seasonal, or it might be that fewer people are finding the page.

⛔ This is the cheapest item on the page and everything else depends on it.

⭐ What is unusual, and in your favour

Seven enquiry forms and twenty-four tap-to-call links across twenty pages. ⭐⭐ That is the best-converting setup of any site we have audited this week, by a distance. Most trade sites have one form and two phone links.

🔴 So you have built a site that is very good at turning readers into callers, and you have no idea how many it turns. That is a frustrating place to be and it is entirely fixable.

⚠️ There is a placeholder email still in the template

Your site publishes name@example.com alongside your real address. That is dummy text from whoever built it, left in.

⚠️ Harmless in itself. ⭐ But it is the sort of thing that tells a careful commercial client nobody has looked at the site in a while, and careful commercial clients are who buy testing work.

⚠️ A Gmail address and a mobile, on a company with three employees

Your published contact is fgelectricalni@gmail.com and a mobile number. You own fgelectricalltd.co.uk.

⚠️ For domestic work that is fine. ⭐ For the commercial and landlord testing contracts you want to double, it reads smaller than you are.

⚠️ No local business details in the code

No address, postcode, opening hours or coordinates where search engines read them. For “electrician near me” and “EICR near me” that is a real gap, and it is an hour or two of work.

The Size Of What You Are Asking For

Doubling the testing work, on your own numbers

20–25 a week → 40–50 a week

That is another 20 to 25 tests a week, or roughly 1,000 to 1,300 more a year. ⚠️ We do not know what you charge for a test, so we are not going to guess at the revenue. That number is the first thing we would ask for.

⚠️ And the question underneath it: could you deliver fifty a week? Three on the books and two or three subbies is a real team, but doubling testing volume is a scheduling job as much as a marketing one. ⭐ If the answer is no, we would say so.
⭐ What Is Already Good
⭐ Do not change these

NICEIC shown on the site. Twenty pages covering EICR and fixed wire testing, EV charging, commercial work, a team page, a portfolio, reviews and insurance. Seven forms and twenty-four tap-to-call links.

⭐⭐ Somebody built this properly. The conversion side is genuinely strong and we would not touch it. What is missing is the measurement around it and the small credibility details.

What We Would Want To Ask
  • 1. What does a test actually earn you? ⭐ The number we need before any of the rest means anything.
  • 2. Where do the 20 to 25 come from now? Landlords, agents, commercial contracts, word of mouth?
  • 3. Could you physically do fifty a week? With three on the books and two or three subbies.
  • 4. On the EV side, what makes you think it is slowing? Fewer enquiries, or fewer converting? ⚠️ They are different problems.
  • 5. Have you ever run advertising? There is no tag on the site, which usually means no.
  • 6. Is the testing work or the EV work the one you actually want more of?
Question 1 unlocks everything else, and ⭐ question 4 is the one you probably cannot answer yet, which is the whole argument for putting measurement on first.

Twenty-four ways to ring you. Nothing counting who does.

You said the EV flow feels like it is slowing. Nothing on your site can tell you whether it is.
